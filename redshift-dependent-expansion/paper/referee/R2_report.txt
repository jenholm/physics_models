## Owner assessment of the report

This referee is useful. Most methodological criticisms are taken
seriously, but they do not force a new cosmological model. The biggest
problems are manuscript architecture and claim semantics, not the
underlying frozen evidence.

### VALID (accepted, repair authorized)

1. R/Model-A identity ambiguity (H0 72.80 vs 73.21 used
   interchangeably). The most important fix.
2. C2's weak scientific independence (same Planck sky, nearly same
   LCDM family) — downgrade to reference-sensitivity check.
3. r_d viability buried (135.39 Mpc, ~8% smaller ruler, no
   BBN/full-CMB validation) — confront directly.
4. Lyα/order-selection inconsistency (catastrophic z=2.33 fold choosing
   the order) — re-score constrained domain, keep Lyα as failed
   continuation test.
5. Rank definition ("effective observable rank 5 of 5" reads as five
   measured quantities) — replace with explicit Jacobian-rank statement.
6. Missing likelihood details (bare null undefined, SN row selection
   unreproducible, distance-duality citation, Chen-prior scope).

### PARTLY VALID

- AIC criticism (mathematically true, not an error: AIC is supposed to
  penalize parameter count) — defang, remove from abstract, report
  descriptively.
- "Whole result is null-consistent" conflation — the quoted bootstrap
  P(select>=m3)=0.148 tests polynomial-complexity selection, not
  existence of the endpoint separation. Tone down claims without
  falsely relabeling the result a null.

### STALE (regression-tested, otherwise ignored)

- 23-parameter vector repeated-q10 complaint: source already has the
  correct unique slots (q_l0..2, q_rho0..2).
- "deliberately" typo complaint: already spelled correctly.
