\section{Model-development history M24--M28}
\label{sec:history}

The M24 flexible time-current discovery established that low-redshift
current structure can move the joint likelihood. The M24E cycle
replaced the compressed local anchor with measurement-level ladder
machinery and audited the observer-frame forward model. The M25/M26
program then built the variable-epoch chronogeometric family under the
exact joint DESI, compressed-CMB, and Pantheon+SH0ES likelihood. This
selected the 10-mode late-time basis definition. The M27 differential-coupling
search was closed as not useful. The early-epoch chronogeometric model
adds one early-epoch degree of freedom $A_{E}$ to the existing
22-parameter late-time family and jointly refits all 23 parameters to
the unchanged likelihood, with the 23-parameter best fit subsequently
frozen, adding the single early amplitude of Eq.~\eqref{eq:m28-piecewise}. The ceiling
formulation first demonstrated the lever in amplitude-only form. The
physical $A_{E}$ parameterization reproduces it with standard
late-time restoration.
