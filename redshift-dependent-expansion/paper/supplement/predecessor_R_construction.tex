\section{Predecessor endpoint-construction optimizer (supplement)}
\label{sec:predecessor-R}

This supplement preserves the historical 23-parameter predecessor
optimizer that produced the endpoint lineage. It is not part of the
main analysis, whose only fitted objects are the bridge coordinates
(Section~\ref{sec:methods}).

The historical search vector was 23-dimensional,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{%
    \theta = (t_{0},\dots,t_{9},\Omega_{m},h_{\mathrm{rd}},
    g_{0},g_{1},x_{1},x_{2},
    q_{l0},q_{l1},q_{l2},q_{\rho0},q_{\rho1},q_{\rho2},A_{E})%
  }
  \label{eq:theta-historical}
\end{equation}
with ten late-time temporal modes, background $(\Omega_{m},h_{\mathrm{rd}})$,
and broad-backbone $(g_{0},g_{1},x_{1},x_{2})$. Duration/width logits
$(q_{l0},q_{l1},q_{l2},q_{\rho0},q_{\rho1},q_{\rho2})$ and the early
amplitude $A_{E}$ completed the vector (23 unique slots; regression
test \texttt{test\_predecessor\_parameter\_vector\_has\_23\_unique\_slots}
locks this down).

The historical objective was the real joint likelihood with no $H_{0}$ target,
penalty, or reweighting:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{%
    \chi^{2}_{\mathrm{real}}
    = \chi^{2}_{\mathrm{DESI}} + \chi^{2}_{\mathrm{CMB}}
    + \chi^{2}_{\mathrm{joint}}%
  }
  \label{eq:chireal-historical}
\end{equation}
where $\chi^{2}_{\mathrm{joint}}$ is the one-common-$M$ 1657-row
Pantheon+SH0ES term.

The historical engine repaired or injected starts into the valid
domain, ran global differential evolution \citep{Storn1997} with QMC
population multiplier 16 \citep{Sobol1967}, tolerance $10^{-4}$, and
polish off, then multistart bounded SLSQP \citep{Kraft1988} with
maximum 500 iterations and ftol $10^{-7}$. Late-time basis evaluation
used spline interpolation \citep{deBoor1978} implemented in
\textsc{Python} with \textsc{NumPy} \citep{Harris2020} and
\textsc{SciPy} \citep{Virtanen2020}. All candidates were retained in a
candidate bank so local polish could not destroy the best solution.
The $A_{E}=0$ same-domain null was the comparator.

\subsection{Predecessor fit-scope details (moved from Limitations)}
\label{sec:predecessor-fit-scope}

The predecessor fit's $\Omega_{m} = \MXXVIIIOmegaM$ lies near the upper
edge of the declared [0.20, 0.45] domain, within the domain with no
extrapolation beyond it. The predecessor publication vector jointly
adjusted all 23 coordinates to the unchanged likelihood; the final
Model~A retains 21 effectively fitted coordinates with $x_{1}=x_{2}=0$
fixed by the Xi-removal repair, so late--early degeneracies beyond the
explored range are not covered. The calibrator result covers the
declared contract only, with $\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{cal}} = 0$ under
validation disposition \textit{calibrator-invariance-assumption-only};
the local-propagation bound ($4.6\times10^{-4}$~mag) does not meet the
$10^{-4}$~mag target, so the assumption is qualitative, not
quantitatively established. Exact-validity aliasing (predecessor vector
only): dense validation gives $\min(1-s)=0.04999183$, missing the
$1-s>0.05$ criterion by $\sim$$8.2\times10^{-6}$. The nearby exact-valid
point recorded in \texttt{configs/m28/epoch\_signature\_models.json}
has $\chi^{2}=1450.553$ (difference $-0.0045$), $H_{0}\sim73.00$,
$r_{d}\sim135.62$, $A_{E}\sim-0.1259$, $\min(1-s)\sim0.098$, with physics
essentially unchanged. Matching at $U=\ln11$ is C0 continuous, not C1.
None of this affects the main paper, whose only fitted objects are the
bridge coordinates.
