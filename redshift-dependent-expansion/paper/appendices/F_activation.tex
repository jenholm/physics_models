\section{Perturbation-channel activation checks}
\label{app:activation}

This appendix records pipeline-hygiene evidence: that the Boltzmann
perturbation channels used in the CMB tests actually respond to their
inputs. It is a methods note, not a physics result.

\paragraph{RGR activation mechanism.} In the EFTCAMB results module the
perturbation switch is overwritten by the RGR time: for models with all
EFT functions zeroed, RGR returns ``never'' (1.1), so EFT perturbations
stay silently off whenever the external background table matches the
solver's own background. Early coupling-rescaling runs therefore
compared identical spectra across parameter values while appearing to
scan. Two fixes removed the artifact: Fortran default initializers for
all working flags (\path{patches/heftcamb/m28_mueta/09_EFTCAMB_defaults.patch},
which also hardens every Python-driven run), and extension of the
designer source rescaling to the stock-GR branch (24 J-sites total),
making activation independent of the RGR switch. The worker path that
clobbered the designer model identifier was repaired at the same time.

\paragraph{Verification.} After the fix: the M28 baseline spectra are
bit-identical to the pre-fix baseline; the GR nesting gate passes
(unlensed spectra agree at $2$--$5\times10^{-7}$ across independent
paths); a $\mu_0=0.2$ coupling moves the TT spectrum by 89\% (channel
engaged); and the CMB-only coupling fit finds its minimum at the GR
edge ($\mu_0=0.037$, $-0.9\%$ in high-$\ell$ only, lowTT non-finite
everywhere). The reconstruction branch used for Model~B2 carries no M28
imports (enforced by AST test) and needs no EFT machinery: GR
perturbations are optimal there (B2 Layer-3).

\paragraph{Reading.} A reviewer cannot dismiss the A/B split as a dead
perturbation channel: the channels demonstrably respond, the B2
observational gates hold (DESI~$<40$, SN~$<1560$, lowTT~$<100$,
high-$\ell$~$<1000$, A high-$\ell$~$>10000$), and the frozen B2 spectra
regenerated independently from the frozen parameters reproduce the
gated likelihoods (high-$\ell$ 583.77 vs 583.77; A 13169.78 exact).
