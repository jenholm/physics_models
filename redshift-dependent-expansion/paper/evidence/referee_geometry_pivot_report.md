# Referee geometry-pivot report (R7-022, hostile conventional-cosmology read)

Scope: rewritten manuscript (common-geometry framing) against frozen
R4V/R5/R6 evidence. Release fails on any unresolved MAJOR item.

## 1. Can every main conclusion be accepted without accepting CGHSTC?
PASS. The four conclusion points quantify frozen-table comparisons
(R vs C1/C2 separations, shared peak near z 0.65, compact-map
goodness with null bootstrap numbers, open physical origin). No
conclusion input is a time-current parameter; Appendix G states the
bridge/triangulation claims do not require that interpretation.

## 2. Are C1 and C2 described accurately as independently constructed?
PASS. Introduction and Section 2 state explicitly: C1 and C2 are
independently constructed branches, not statistically independent
datasets; they share the Planck sky but differ in pipeline,
parameters, and calibration route.

## 3. Best-fit endpoint geometry vs posterior significance?
PASS. Limitations state all values are best fits with no posterior
intervals claimed. The m3 selection is reported with held-out
behavior and null-bootstrap frequencies, never as a posterior
detection.

## 4. Is the Lyman-alpha failure disclosed prominently?
PASS. Abstract (DESI held-out degradation through extrapolation),
Section 8/11 (catastrophic held-out chi2 at high order), Section 11
continuation-test framing with constrained domain z<=1.8, and
discussion non-established list.

## 5. Does the bootstrap prevent a false detection claim?
PASS. P(select>=m3|null)=0.148 and in-sample P=0.120 are stated in
abstract, Section 10 prosecution subsection, discussion, and
conclusion. No sigma is manufactured; strength is assigned to
transfer/triangulation geometry instead.

## 6. Does the paper overstate "geometry" as a metric-theory result?
MINOR (resolved, no text change). The title phrase "Common
Redshift-Dependent Geometry" could in isolation suggest a metric
claim, but every operative use is qualified: "empirical
background-geometry difference", "empirical background
reconstruction", "compression basis, not a fundamental expansion
law", and the conclusion's "empirically defined geometric target".
The preferred title is retained per owner instruction; the fallback
title remains available if the owner judges otherwise.

## 7. Is R's time-current provenance disclosed without becoming the interpretation?
PASS. Section 2 states R originated in the redshift-dependent
time-current search, is frozen and treated empirically, and that the
analyses do not assume that interpretation. Appendix G records the
route; discussion gives time-current one unprivileged slot among
five interpretation categories.

## 8. Any remaining sentence whose truth requires undemonstrated fundamental dynamics?
PASS (no MAJOR). Rendered-text grep for headline-claim patterns
(time-current cosmology, CGHSTC predicts, resolves the Hubble
tension, new physics detected, modified gravity detected, proof of,
fundamental dynamics established) returns zero hits. Remaining
time-current mentions are provenance/discussion/appendix only.

## Verdict
No unresolved MAJOR items. Geometry-pivot referee audit: GREEN.
