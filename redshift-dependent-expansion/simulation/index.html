How to read this page

  • Blue points (R): where each synthetic tracer lands when distances are computed with the distance-sector endpoint history.
  • Red points (C1): where the same tracer lands with the Planck-compatible reference history.
  • Amber halo (C2, off by default): a second Planck-compatible reference; it sits nearly on top of C1 and only checks reference sensitivity.
  • White spokes: the exaggerated radial gap between the two projections of one tracer. Lengths are multiplied for visibility and are not to scale.
  • Bright shell: tracers near the current redshift cursor light up; the same cursor moves on the graphs below.
  • Shading: blue-tinted redshifts (z ≤ 1.8) are the constrained reconstruction domain; amber (1.8 < z ≤ 2.33) is the continuation-test region; beyond that is illustration only.
